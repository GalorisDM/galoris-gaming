Build Flow

Level 1 → Varashta

Level with Grenades through Act 1 and early Act 2, then complete the first Ascendancy around level 21 and convert to the Disciple of Varashta minion/Command setup.

Level 14

Gas Grenade joins the leveling rotation.

Level 21

First Ascendancy, Kelari’s Deception, full passive reset, Sceptre/minion swap.

Level 31–36

Archon of Undeath, then Muster and broader minion variety.
