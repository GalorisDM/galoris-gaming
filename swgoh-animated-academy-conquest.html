How to approach the cycle

Win once, progress several feats

The fastest Conquest route is rarely the strongest possible team. Use squads that advance two or three objectives at once, farm long-duration feats on durable enemies, and rotate key characters before stamina becomes the reason a strategy fails.

Stamina rule:
When possible, split your farming into two play sessions each day. A strong squad at 70–100% stamina is usually more energy-efficient than repeatedly forcing the same team at low stamina.
Progression

Push first, farm second

Reach later sectors early so you have access to better disk choices and more favorable farming nodes. Come back for grind feats once the disk set is mature.

Opponent choice

Farm the right node

Prefer teams that heal, revive, or stall when you need repeated debuffs/buffs. Prefer fragile teams when you are chasing kills or clean wins.

Feat rewards

Keep utility rewards

Booming Voice, F3-4T Training Droids, and Deployable Cooling Systems can turn awkward multi-battle feats into controlled one- or two-battle farms.

Sector 1

DoTs, Retaliate & setup wins

Best early double-dip: KX + ISB

300 Damage Over Time

Primary: Tusken Chief / Warrior / Raider / Shaman / Ur'ro'r'r'ro'r.

Accessible alternative: Emperor Palpatine / Mara Jade / Darth Vader / Darth Vader (Duel's End) / Royal Guard.

Choose a durable opponent and let Vader or the Tuskens stack DoTs instead of ending the battle immediately.

10 Wins with KX Surviving

Primary: Director Partagaz / Dedra Meero / KX / Krennic / Probe Droid.

Carry option: put KX beside a strong Galactic Legend or control core.

One of the Sector 1 boss objectives requires full ISB, so an ISB route naturally reduces the number of extra KX wins.

Gain Retaliate 15 Times

Fast concept: repeatedly call Asajj Ventress (Dark Disciple) to assist; her out-of-turn attacks can build Retaliate.

Alternative: use a Profit/Pirate team that can generate repeated Retaliate stacks.

This is a setup feat—damage is secondary to generating the buff.

Gain 100 Buffs

Normally completes through standard progression. Phoenix, Jedi, Galactic Republic, and Imperial Troopers all generate large buff counts.

Do not spend dedicated energy here unless it is somehow unfinished after clearing the sector.

Mini-boss: BAM + Bo-Katan in squad • Cobb Vanth in squad. Boss: full ISB • no Tanks. CLS, Queen Amidala, or Imperial Troopers are clean no-Tank choices.
Sector 2

Critical Chance Down, Expose & DTMG

Best overlap: DTMG + support-heavy Empire

10 Wins with DTMG

Primary: DTMG / Scout Trooper / Moff Gideon / Night Trooper / Death Trooper (Peridea).

Overlap build: Tarkin / Scout / DTMG plus two non-Attacker Empire supports to also work no-Attacker and debuff feats.

10 Wins with No Attackers

Reliable: Veers / Piett / Moff Gideon / Range Trooper / Starck.

Jedi alternative: JML / Plo Koon / Shaak Ti / Old Ben / Hermit Yoda.

Check roles carefully—the feat evaluates the game's Attacker tag, not how much damage the character deals.

100 Critical Chance Down

Efficient Empire: Tarkin + Scout + DTMG core; Tarkin's mass debuff is the workhorse.

Strong alternative: Baylan / Shin / Marrok / Hondo / Qi'ra; once Shin is ramped, her basic can spread the debuff broadly.

The Critical Debuffs data disk does not satisfy this feat; applications need to come from abilities.

20 Expose

Double-dip: the Tarkin/Scout/DTMG shell can work Expose while earning DTMG wins.

Alternatives: Zorii Resistance • Rogue One • ISB.

Mini-boss: Darth Vader (Duel's End) in squad • no Empire. Boss: The Stranger solo • Stormtrooper Luke surviving.

Progress naturally with your safest teams. Avoid farming this at low stamina; a single death wastes both energy and progress opportunity.

Primary: Satele Shan / JKR / Bastila / Juhani / Jolee.

Alternative: JML/Jedi shell.

Jedi Lessons is roster-gated; if you do not have a character who can grant it, plan your crate target around skipping these points.

Primary: General Grievous / MagnaGuard / B2 / B1 / STAP.

Alternative: ISB with KX. STAP and KX are the key Overcharge engines.

DCS can make a controlled Overcharge farm much easier.

Mini-boss: Kaz surviving • Dark Side only. Boss: Satele surviving • no Force Users. Separatist Droids, ISB, Jabba, or Mandalorians are strong no-Force options.

Sector 5

Buff Disruption, Tactical Supremacy & Potency Up

Most valuable place for controlled cheese

30 Buff Disruption

Key unit: Stormtrooper Luke. Build a stall team that keeps him alive and repeatedly gives him natural turns to use his Basic.

Fast farm: CLS/Han/Chewie/Chewpio/Stormtrooper Luke against a damage-immune target can create a long safe loop.

Calling Stormtrooper Luke to assist does not replace the need for his own-turn Basic, so prioritize Turn Meter/cooldown control over extra assists.

Gain Tactical Supremacy 25 Times

ISB: Partagaz / Dedra / KX / Probe / Krennic.

Separatists: Trench / Wat / Nute / Dooku / GBA.

Empire: Palpatine / Mara Jade / Vader (Duel's End) / Tarkin / Royal Guard.

10 Wins with Boba + Jango

Best carry: Jabba / Krrsantan / Boushh / Boba / Jango.

Accessible: Bossk / Boba / Jango / Greef / Mandalorian—pick favorable nodes because Sector 5 modifiers can overwhelm classic Bounty Hunters.

Gain Potency Up 50 Times

Alternative: Baylan/Mercenary core or Jedi with Kit Fisto + GMY buff spread.

Mini-boss: Cassian Andor (Undercover) in squad • full Galactic Republic. Boss: Jedi Training Rey surviving • full Inquisitors surviving.
Current-cycle disk board

Data disk priorities

Ranked for a mix of clear speed, survival, feat control, and capacity efficiency. A lower-ranked disk can still be best when it directly enables the feat you are farming.

S

Take on Sight

Build-around disks with exceptional return

Zealous Ambition2 capacity

Turns Supports and Healers into nukes based on Max Health. Multiple copies stack into one of the safest high-damage setups.

Desolation2 capacity

Ramps non-Attacker Offense and non-Tank Max Health whenever enemies lose Turn Meter or gain DoTs. Outstanding with Caustic Emissions and Unstable Decelerator.

Unstable Decelerator2 capacity

Turn Meter removal on damage provides control and continuously feeds Desolation.

Disruptive Reactions4 capacity

Enemy turns feed 100% Turn Meter to a random ally. Expensive, but can break hostile Conquest speed/TM loops.

Premonition4 capacity

Buff-heavy teams gain Foresight, and evades reduce cooldowns. Excellent with Evasive Technique and Heal Over Time.

A

Very Strong

High-value support pieces and control

Guard and Penetrate3 capacity

Huge opening Defense plus scaling penetration. Excellent later-sector insurance.

Heal Over Time1 capacity

Extremely efficient buff generation and sustain; also fuels Premonition and other buff-trigger disks.

Evasive Technique2 capacity

Provides Evasion Up and Turn Meter on evade. Strongest paired with Premonition.

Caustic Emissions1 capacity

Cheap automatic DoTs that power Desolation and debuff-count control packages.

Debuff Dismantler2 capacity

Buff gains can cleanse the ally and stack Mastery. Great on teams that generate buffs constantly.

Ability Exhaust + Stun Exhaustcontrol package

With enough automatic debuffs, enemies become repeatedly Ability Blocked and Stunned.

B

Useful / Flexible

Good fillers when the primary package is already built

Booming Voicefeat reward

Excellent assist engine and required for its global feat. Great with leaders who take frequent turns.

Solid Intelsurvival/TM

Helps teams recover momentum after critical hits and adds useful offensive buffs.

Undercoversurvival

Emergency Health/Protection recovery helps carry weak feat characters.

Opportunistic Supportassist sustain

Useful on teams with frequent assists and counterattacks.

Mortal Strikeanti-heal

Good into high-healing nodes; creates bonus-turn pressure while Healing Immunity is active.

Perseveranceassist damage

Extra out-of-turn attacks can be strong, but the damage penalty means it is not universal.

C

Niche / Do Not Chase

Use for a specific job, not as the core plan

Critical Debuffsniche

Good general control, but its automatic Critical Chance Down does not count for the Sector 2 application feat.

Caustic Recurrence4 capacity

The once-per-turn restriction makes it harder to justify over cheaper, more predictable DoT/ramp options.

Defensive Formationteam-dependent

Solid for assist-heavy teams but usually a secondary pickup unless survivability is the limiting factor.

Special feat tools: F3-4T Training Droids is not a normal “clear faster” choice—it is a farming tool that can keep enemies alive while you stack buffs/debuffs. DCS is similarly valuable because cooldown reduction extends long feat loops. Use these intentionally, then switch back to your normal clear set.
Recommended packages

Data disk loadouts

Think in packages rather than individual disks. Fill the remaining capacity with survivability or feat-specific tools.

01

Support Nuke

ZA ×3Guard & PenetrateHeal Over Time

Simple and devastating. Supports/Healers gain huge damage while the defensive pieces keep them alive long enough to take over.

Best for: general progression, fast three-star clears, weaker support-heavy teams.
02

Control & Ramp

DesolationCaustic EmissionsUnstable Decelerator

Every hit pushes enemy Turn Meter backward while automatic DoTs and TM loss stack your team's Offense/Health.

Best for: long feat farms, undergeared teams, debuff-heavy squads.
03

Debuff Lockdown

Critical Debuffs/CEAbility ExhaustStun Exhaust

Stack enough automatic debuffs to repeatedly Ability Block and Stun enemies. Prioritize control over raw damage.

Best for: farming repeated applications while preventing dangerous enemy turns.
04

Evasion Loop

PremonitionEvasive TechniqueHeal Over Time ×2

HoTs generate buffs, buffs generate Foresight, evades generate Turn Meter and cooldown reduction. Add ZA or Desolation with remaining capacity.

Best for: cooldown-heavy squads and hostile high-Speed sectors.
