Competitive Growth

Academy of DSA Legends

Focus: Competitive growth and coordination to achieve without things feeling like a job.

600k raid minimum600 ticketsDiscord required

Expectations

  • Active participation in guild events.
  • TW: Sign up only if you can participate and set the requested number of defensive squads.
  • TB: Follow orders in the DSA TB Orders Discord channel.
  • Raids: Minimum raid score of 600,000.
  • Work toward raid-viable and TB-focused farms.
  • 600/600 daily raid tickets.
  • Discord activity is required; be responsive to officer pings.
  • Register with the C-3P0, Mahandalorian, and Echostation bots. Officers can help with setup.
  • More than 2 days inactive without notice will result in a warning and likely removal.
Growth

Ammophobia

Focus: Growing and progressing with moderate expectations.

300k raid minimum600 ticketsDiscord required

Expectations

  • Active participation in guild events.
  • TW: Sign up if you can set at least 2 defensive squads.
  • TB: Follow orders in the Ammo TB Orders Discord channel.
  • Raids: Minimum raid score of 300,000.
  • Work toward raid-viable and TB-focused farms.
  • 600/600 daily raid tickets.
  • Discord activity is required; be responsive to officer pings.
  • Register with the C-3P0 bot by using /allycode add 123456789 with your own ally code. Officers can help if needed.
  • More than 3 days inactive without notice will result in a warning and likely removal.
