Zeta & Omicron Priorities

Spend scarce ability materials where they change teams the most. These tiers are an alliance-oriented shortlist collated from current community rankings, ability-use patterns, game-mode impact, and alliance utility—not a rule that every account must follow blindly.

How to use this list

Build the team, then buy the upgrade

A “Must Have” ability is only immediate when you own the character and are actively building or using the team. Finish requirements that unlock a team first; do not spend a Zeta or Omicron just because it appears high on a global list.

Alliance rule of thumb:
For Zetas, prioritize abilities that make a team function. For Omicrons, prioritize the game modes you actually play and the squads your guild relies on. TW/TB Omicrons can be more valuable to the alliance than a personal GAC upgrade.

Omicron order suggestions

Omicron priorities

Mode tags matter. An Omicron that is incredible in GAC does nothing in TW or TB.

Immediate Must Have

High return when you own the corresponding team

Padawan Obi-Wan CONQUEST
Nobility in Restraint
Transformative in Conquest: boosts the whole squad's durability and Speed, repeatedly creates bonus-turn opportunities, restores allies, and floods the battle with buffs and debuffs. If Conquest progression matters to you, this belongs at the very top.
The Stranger GAC
I Want a Pupil / You Might Call Me... Sith / An Acolyte Kills the Dream
If the Stranger team is built, these are team-defining GAC upgrades rather than luxury additions. Prioritize the core team package before secondary roster Omicrons.
Rey (Dark Side Vision) GAC

A complete modern GAC package. Once the character is unlocked and actively used, her Omicrons belong ahead of broad convenience investments.
